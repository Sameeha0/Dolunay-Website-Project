Cast & characters

The people of Dolunay

A perfectionist, a chef, a little matchmaker, and the friends and family who make every dinner complicated.

Ferit Aslan

Played by Can Yaman

A wealthy, well-known businessman who keeps his life in perfect order. Ever since his mother's affair he has distrusted people, especially women. Taking in his orphaned nephew Bulut forces him to open his home, and his heart.

LeadBusinessmanBulut's uncle

Nazmiye “Nazlı” Pınar

Played by Özge Gürel

A passionate culinary student who dreams of owning her own restaurant. Cheerful and stubborn, she becomes Ferit's private chef while juggling the responsibility of her younger sister Asuman.

LeadChefAsuman's sister

Bulut Kaya

Played by Alihan Türkdemir

Demir and Zeynep's son and Ferit's nephew, whom Ferit tries to adopt. He is very fond of Nazlı and often tries to set her up with his uncle.

FamilyFerit's nephew

Asuman Pınar

Played by İlayda Akdoğan

Nazlı's mischievous younger sister, a tourism student who would rather hang out with friends than go to class. Wanting to live like the rich, she runs up debts, and Demet uses her need for money to sabotage Ferit.

FamilyNazlı's sister

Fatoş Yalçın

Played by Öznur Serçeler

Nazlı's best friend and roommate, an aspiring but unemployed fashion designer. She pretends to be wealthy to impress Engin.

FriendFashion designer

Deniz Kaya

Played by Hakan Kurtaş

Demet and Demir's brother and Ferit's close friend. A carefree, emotional musician who falls for Nazlı the moment he meets her, and whose jealousy starts to come between him and Ferit.

FriendMusicianRival
